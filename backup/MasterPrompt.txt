Maps & Path SystemThree maps. Player provides JPG backgrounds.
Use a grid / tilemap system on top of the JPG so paths can be easily adjusted to match the roads on the image.
Each map has 5 spawn points.
Dynamic pathfinding: enemies path around towers.
Restricted placement zones near path edges so the path can never be fully blocked (allows funneling but prevents abuse).
Map 1: 10 waves  
Map 2: Unlocked at Wave 10 of Map 1 (10 waves)  
Map 3: Unlocked at Wave 10 of Map 2 (10 waves)  
Endless mode unlocked after clearing each map.

Towers (6 total)Slayer (Melee) – Cone attack in front of the tower  
Spearman (Melee) – Directional attack, smaller area, longer range  
Striker (Melee) – Area attack around itself  
Marksman (Ranged) – Targets enemy closest to the Fort, straight projectile  
Outpost (Ranged) – Projectile that deals area damage on impact  
Balista (Ranged) – Straight piercing projectile, targets highest HP enemy

Towers have hitboxes and can be placed on the path (within rules).Economy & Skill Tree (XP only)Player starts with 30 XP.
XP is earned by killing enemies and is used exclusively on the Skill Tree.
Starting nodes (10 XP each): Slayer, Spearman, Striker, Marksman.
Outpost and Balista are unlocked deeper in the tree.
Each of the four starting towers has two branches:Slayer: Bleeding on hit / AoE size
Spearman: Damage / Attack Range
Striker: Damage / AoE size
Marksman: Damage / Projectile Speed

Skill tree nodes can also increase the maximum number of a specific tower.
Refund is only possible through a special refund node on the skill tree (select talents to refund).
No selling towers during Placement Phase.
